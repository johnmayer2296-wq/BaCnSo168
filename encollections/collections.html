BANIBUBA HERITAGE COLLECTION FAQ

Find answers about our cultural collections, featured products, pricing, availability, shipping options, and small quantity inquiries.

Banibuba shares meaningful Chinese products through stories, collections, and cultural discoveries. Since many featured items come from different suppliers and creative sources in China, availability, pricing, and shipping details may vary.

If you discover a product you like, simply contact us with the product SKU number. We will help confirm the latest information and guide you through the next steps.
About Banibuba Collections

What are Banibuba Collections?
Banibuba Collections are curated collections of Chinese cultural products, traditional objects, creative designs, and meaningful items discovered from China.
Through stories, images, and cultural backgrounds, we introduce products that reflect Chinese aesthetics, craftsmanship, symbolism, and everyday creativity.
Some products are selected from Chinese suppliers and markets, while others are developed through our own creative ideas and studio exploration.


Is Banibuba an online shopping platform?
No. Banibuba Collections is not a traditional e-commerce store.
We focus on discovering meaningful products and sharing the stories behind them. Customers can contact us directly if they are interested in a featured product.

Product Inquiry & Ordering

Can I purchase products shown on the Collections pages?
Yes. Selected products displayed in Collections stories and collections are available for inquiry and purchase.
Each product has a unique SKU number.

Please provide:
• Product SKU
• Product name
• Required quantity
• Shipping country

We will confirm:
• Product availability
• Latest product price
• Shipping cost
• Estimated delivery time


What is the minimum order quantity?
For most selected products, the minimum order quantity can be 1 piece.
However, availability depends on product type and supplier conditions.
Some handmade or special products may require additional confirmation.

Pricing & Product Information

Are the prices shown on the website final?
No. The prices displayed on Banibuba Collections pages are reference prices based on recent China domestic market information.
Prices may change due to:
• Supplier updates
• Material costs
• Market changes
• Product availability
• Exchange rates
The final product price will be confirmed before payment.


Does the product price include international shipping?
No. The displayed price only refers to the product itself.
International shipping is calculated separately according to:
• Destination country
• Package weight
• Product size
• Shipping method
The latest shipping cost and estimated delivery time will be confirmed before payment.

Quality Confirmation & Product Preparation

Are products checked before shipping?
For selected products, especially handmade items, customized items, or products with unique details, we may provide photos or videos before shipment.
This allows customers to confirm important details before delivery.


Are all products kept in stock?
No. Banibuba does not maintain large inventory for Collections products.
Many products are purchased after customer confirmation, which allows us to offer more diverse collections without unnecessary stock.


Do you support large quantity orders?
Collections focus mainly on individual purchases and small quantity orders.
For larger quantities or business projects, please contact us separately to discuss possibilities.

Shipping, Delivery & After-Sales

How is shipping arranged?
After confirming your order, we will arrange international shipping based on your destination and product information.
Shipping costs and delivery time are calculated separately.
Before payment, we will provide:
• Shipping cost
• Estimated delivery time
• Shipping method information


How long does delivery take?
Delivery time depends on:
• Destination country
• Shipping method
• Product size and weight
• Logistics conditions
Estimated delivery time will be provided before payment.


What if the product arrives damaged or incorrect?
If there is a problem with your order, please contact us with photos or videos.
We will review the situation and communicate with suppliers or related parties to find the best possible solution.

Payment & Customer Support

What payment methods are available?
Currently, Banibuba accepts:
• PayPal
• Alipay

Before payment, we will confirm:
• Product details
• Product price
• Shipping cost
• Estimated delivery information


Why is payment required before purchasing?
Most Collections products are sourced after customer confirmation.
Payment allows us to arrange product purchasing, preparation, and shipping.
For small quantity orders, full payment is normally required before purchasing.


Is payment safe?
Before payment, we confirm all important details including:
• Product information
• Total cost
• Shipping details
We maintain clear communication throughout the process.
