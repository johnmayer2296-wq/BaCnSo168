01. How We Support Your Sourcing Journey

What does Banibuba do?
Banibuba provides independent sourcing support for international buyers who want to explore products and supply chain opportunities in China.
We help clients understand product options, communicate with suppliers, evaluate sourcing possibilities, and coordinate local purchasing activities.
Our role is to support buyers during the sourcing process rather than act as a product seller.


Are you a trading company?
No. We do not operate as a traditional trading company.
We do not purchase products and resell them with hidden margins. Our focus is providing sourcing assistance, local communication, and procurement support based on buyers’ requirements.


Why do international buyers need local sourcing support?
China has a large and complex supply chain. For overseas buyers, challenges often come from communication differences, supplier understanding, product evaluation, and local coordination.
Local support helps buyers save time, reduce uncertainty, and make better sourcing decisions.


Who can benefit from this service?
Our service is suitable for small businesses, online sellers, retailers, emerging brands, and companies that want to explore Chinese suppliers without building their own local team.


Do you represent buyers or suppliers?
Our service is designed from the buyer’s perspective.
We focus on understanding buyer requirements and helping them communicate more effectively with suitable supply resources.


What makes your service different?
Our value is not only finding products. We focus on improving communication, increasing transparency, and helping buyers better understand the Chinese supply chain.

02. Product Categories & Sourcing Scope

What product categories do you focus on?
Our main focus covers six consumer product categories:
Accessories, Textiles, Gadgets, Pet Supplies, Home Essentials, and Festive Decor.
We concentrate on these areas to build deeper product understanding and sourcing experience.


Can you help with products outside these categories?
Our main expertise is within the six categories above. For products outside these areas, we may evaluate requests depending on the product type and available resources.


Why do you focus on specific categories?
A focused approach allows us to understand suppliers, product characteristics, and market opportunities more effectively.


How do you select suppliers?
We prioritize manufacturers and original supply sources whenever suitable. We evaluate suppliers based on product experience, production capability, quality requirements, communication efficiency, and cooperation needs. The right supplier is not always the largest factory, but the one that best matches the buyer’s project.


Do you help buyers find the cheapest suppliers?
Our goal is not simply finding the lowest price.
We help buyers balance cost, quality, reliability, and long-term cooperation value.


Do you provide supplier information directly?
Supplier information is only one part of sourcing.
The more important part is understanding whether the supplier matches the buyer’s requirements and how communication and cooperation can be managed effectively.

03. Supplier Communication & Verification

How do you help buyers communicate with Chinese suppliers?
Communication is one of the most important parts of sourcing from China.
We help buyers communicate product requirements, clarify specifications, discuss questions, and reduce misunderstandings caused by language or business culture differences.


Can you help evaluate whether a supplier is suitable?
Yes. Supplier suitability depends on many factors, including product experience, communication response, production capability, business requirements, and cooperation expectations.
We help buyers collect and understand relevant information to support better sourcing decisions.


Do you only work with manufacturers?
Not necessarily.
Different products have different supply chain structures. Some projects may require direct manufacturers, while others may involve specialized production partners or other suitable local supply resources.
Our focus is selecting the most suitable sourcing approach based on the buyer’s needs.


How do you reduce the risk of working with unknown suppliers?
Before cooperation, it is important to understand supplier information, product details, communication quality, and business conditions.
We help buyers improve information transparency and identify potential issues before making important decisions.


Can you communicate with suppliers on behalf of buyers?
Yes. We can assist with communication during the sourcing process, including requirement discussions, product questions, clarification of details, and coordination between both sides.


Can you help compare different sourcing options?
Yes. Different suppliers and sourcing methods may have different advantages.
We help buyers understand available options and evaluate them based on cost, product requirements, flexibility, and cooperation conditions.

04. Sourcing Process & Workflow

How does the sourcing process usually start?
The process usually starts with a simple conversation.
Buyers can share product ideas, images, links, specifications, or business goals. Based on the information provided, we discuss possible sourcing directions and suitable next steps.


What information do you need from buyers?
Useful information includes product images, references, expected quantity, target market, quality requirements, and any special requests.
The clearer the requirements, the more efficiently suitable sourcing options can be explored.


Do buyers need to know exactly what they want?
Not always.
Some buyers already have specific products, while others are exploring new opportunities. We can discuss ideas and help organize information before moving into detailed sourcing.


How long does a sourcing project usually take?
The timeline depends on product complexity, supplier availability, communication requirements, and order details. Simple product discussions may move quickly, while more detailed projects require more time for evaluation and coordination.


Do you support one-time projects?
Yes. Some buyers need support for a specific product or project, while others require ongoing sourcing assistance. We can discuss cooperation based on actual needs.


Can this become a long-term cooperation?
Yes. Many sourcing relationships develop through successful communication and project experience. Long-term cooperation allows both sides to understand expectations better and improve efficiency over time.

05. Samples & Product Confirmation

Can I request samples before a larger order?
Yes, when sample options are available from suppliers.
Samples are usually arranged directly according to supplier conditions. We can assist with communication regarding availability, product details, and related arrangements.


Who pays for samples and shipping costs?
Sample costs, domestic transportation, and international shipping fees are normally paid according to the arrangement between the buyer and supplier.
The specific cost depends on the product type, supplier policy, and shipping method.


Can you check products before shipment?
Depending on the project requirements, we can assist with product information confirmation, supplier communication, photos, videos, and other available methods.
For professional inspections or on-site checks, additional arrangements may be discussed according to project needs.


Why is product confirmation important?
Product images and descriptions may not always show every detail.
Confirming materials, specifications, packaging, and expectations before ordering helps reduce misunderstandings and improves purchasing confidence.


Do all products need samples?
Not necessarily.
For standard products from reliable suppliers, buyers may make decisions based on their own requirements. For new products or new suppliers, samples can provide additional confidence.


Can you help with product improvement suggestions?
When appropriate, we can share market observations and communication feedback to help buyers better understand product possibilities and supplier capabilities.

06. Pricing & Cost Optimization

Can you help buyers understand product pricing in China?
Yes. Product prices in China can vary depending on materials, production methods, supplier experience, order quantity, customization requirements, and other factors.
We help buyers better understand pricing factors and evaluate whether a quotation is reasonable based on the actual product situation.


Can you help reduce sourcing costs?
Our goal is not simply to find the lowest price, but to help buyers improve overall sourcing efficiency.
By understanding product requirements, comparing suitable options, and improving communication with suppliers, buyers may discover opportunities to optimize costs.


Why are prices from different suppliers sometimes very different?
Different quotations may reflect differences in materials, quality standards, production processes, service levels, packaging, or business models.
A lower price does not always mean a better choice. The right option depends on the buyer’s actual goals.


Can you negotiate prices with suppliers?
Yes. When appropriate, we can assist with communication regarding product details, order requirements, and commercial discussions.
However, the final agreement depends on the buyer and supplier’s direct cooperation.


Do you guarantee the lowest supplier price?
No. The lowest price is not always the best sourcing solution.
We focus on helping buyers find suitable suppliers with a balance of cost, quality, reliability, and long-term cooperation potential.


Can you help compare different supplier quotations?
Yes. We can help buyers understand differences between quotations and evaluate factors such as product specifications, materials, quantity requirements, and cooperation conditions.

07. Shipping & Delivery

Do you arrange international shipping?
Banibuba focuses on sourcing support and supplier coordination rather than operating as a shipping company.
Shipping arrangements are usually handled between buyers and professional logistics providers. We can assist with communication and understanding available options when needed.


Can you help estimate shipping costs?
Shipping costs depend on product weight, volume, destination country, shipping method, and market conditions.
We can help buyers collect relevant information, while final quotations should be confirmed with logistics providers.


How long does international shipping usually take?
Delivery time varies depending on the shipping method, destination, customs procedures, and logistics conditions.
Actual delivery schedules should be confirmed according to the selected logistics solution.


Can you help communicate delivery requirements with suppliers?
Yes. We can assist buyers in confirming production schedules, packaging requirements, delivery information, and communication details with suppliers.


What happens if there are delays?
Delays can happen due to production schedules, material availability, holidays, transportation, or other unexpected factors.
We help improve communication between buyers and suppliers so issues can be identified and addressed more efficiently.


Do you provide tracking information?
Tracking information is normally provided by logistics providers or suppliers according to the selected shipping method.
We can assist with communication if buyers need support understanding delivery progress.

08. Quality Control & Risk Management

How do you help reduce sourcing risks?
Sourcing from China involves many factors, including supplier understanding, communication, product expectations, and coordination.
Our role is to improve transparency, support communication, and help buyers make more informed decisions.


Can you assist with pre-shipment inspections?
Yes. If required, we can provide on-site assistance before shipment, including quantity checking, product condition review, packaging confirmation, and photo or video updates.
The inspection scope will be confirmed with the buyer and supplier in advance based on the product type, location, and project requirements. We provide on-site support and feedback based on the actual situation at the time of inspection.
To ensure accuracy, any changes after inspection, including repacking, additional loading, or further handling by suppliers, should be confirmed between buyers and suppliers.

Can you help verify supplier information?
Yes. We can assist buyers in understanding supplier background information, communication situation, product experience, and other relevant factors.


What are common risks when sourcing from China?
Common challenges include unclear communication, unsuitable suppliers, inconsistent expectations, quality misunderstandings, and inefficient coordination.
Proper preparation and local support can help reduce these risks.


Can you help solve communication problems with suppliers?
Yes. We can assist with communication, clarification of requirements, and coordination when misunderstandings or difficulties occur.


Can sourcing support help reduce procurement risks?
Our role is to help reduce sourcing risks, not simply find suppliers. By maintaining close communication with suppliers, following project progress, and providing timely updates from China, we help buyers identify potential issues earlier and make sourcing decisions with greater confidence.
Final purchasing decisions always remain with the buyer.

10. Service Fees & Payment Transparency

How does cooperation and payment work?
Banibuba provides independent sourcing support services for international buyers.
Our fees are based on the actual sourcing assistance provided, including supplier communication, product research, information coordination, and local support.
We do not purchase products for resale, add hidden margins, or earn supplier commissions.


Why do you charge service fees?
Finding suitable suppliers and managing sourcing communication in China requires time, local knowledge, and continuous coordination.
Service fees allow us to provide independent support and focus on helping buyers make better sourcing decisions.


Do you make money from product price differences?
No. Our business model is based on transparent service fees rather than product markups or supplier rebates.
This helps us maintain an independent position and focus on the buyer’s requirements.


Who pays the product supplier?
Product payments are normally arranged directly between buyers and suppliers according to the agreed purchasing terms.
Our role is to support communication, coordination, and sourcing management.


What payment methods do you accept for service fees?
For service fees, we currently support international payment methods such as PayPal and Alipay.
Before starting paid services, we confirm the service scope, expected work, and payment details clearly.


Can we start with a small project?
Yes. Starting with a smaller project allows both sides to understand the working process, communication style, and cooperation expectations before developing a longer-term relationship.
