SOURCING SERVICES

For small-batch orders, we handle procurement on your behalf, removing minimum order quantity barriers so you can secure competitive pricing even with smaller volumes. For large-volume orders, we connect you directly with primary source factories, and you sign contracts and transact directly with them. We do not mark up product prices; our service fees are transparent and clearly stated. We serve simply as your reliable procurement partner in China.

SUPPLIER SCREENING & EVALUATION

Based on your requirements, we match 3–5 quality suppliers from our database of verified source factories. We conduct background checks, analyze export data, and compare quotes to deliver a complete evaluation report — helping you skip middlemen and connect directly with real manufacturers. 

FACTORY VERIFICATION SUPPORT

Through document reviews, video inspections, and on-site visits, we thoroughly assess each factory's actual production capacity, working environment, and quality control systems. We identify risks early so you don't pay for fake suppliers. 

FULL-PROCESS PROCUREMENT COORDINATION

From RFQ preparation to shipment coordination, we follow through every step — specification confirmation, price negotiation, production scheduling, quality inspection, and shipping. Every stage is traceable, ensuring stable delivery, controlled quality, and transparent information. 

SUPPLY CHAIN CONSULTING

As your long-term partner in China, we provide ongoing market trend analysis, cost optimization strategies, alternative factory options, and category expansion advice — helping you build a stable, sustainable supply chain in China.
