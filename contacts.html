LOCAL PRESENCE ACROSS CHINA

Rather than operating from a single office, Banibuba works as a flexible sourcing support team based in China. We work directly with manufacturers and suppliers, providing local expertise, factory coordination, supplier communication, and project support throughout the sourcing process. Our focus is simple: to be where our clients need us most and help them source with greater confidence.

Whether your sourcing project is just an idea or already in progress, Banibuba is here to provide practical advice and local support. TELL US ABOUT YOUR PROJECT

To help us better understand your sourcing needs, please share a few details about your project when contacting our team. You don't need a complete sourcing plan — even an initial idea is a great place to start.

  1. PRODUCT: Share the product name, category, or specifications you are looking for.
  2. REQUIREMENTS: Tell us your technical requirements, quality standards, or special expectations.
  3. QUANTITY: Provide your estimated order volume, purchasing plan, or business goals.
  4. MARKET: Let us know your target country, industry, or application needs.
  5. CHALLENGES: Explain your current sourcing difficulties or areas where you need support.

QUESTIONS BEFORE GETTING STARTED

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. The initial discussion and requirement assessment are completely free. We first focus on understanding your needs and exploring possible solutions.

No. Banibuba provides independent sourcing support. We do not hold inventory, resell products, or add hidden markups to products.
