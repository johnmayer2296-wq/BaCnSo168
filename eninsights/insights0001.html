Intertextile 2026 Shanghai Spring/Summer Home Textiles Fair – Sourcing Review Guide

The 2026 Shanghai Spring/Summer Home Textiles Fair was held from March 11–13 at the National Exhibition and Convention Center (Shanghai). This year's fair was organized around seven domestic industry clusters plus an international brand pavilion, with each zone dedicated to specific product categories, production capabilities, and export buyer profiles.


I. Seven Domestic Industry Cluster Zones – Category Positioning & Export Fit

Nantong Zone (65 exhibitors – Bedding Core Cluster)
• Core Products: High-count cotton/lyocell bedding sets, hotel linens, printed bedding fabrics, quilted upholstery fabrics
• Best For: Overseas wholesalers, hotel & hospitality buyers, full-container bulk order buyers
• Export Advantages: Large-scale production capacity, OEKO-TEX/flame-retardant certifications widely held, focused on minimalist solid-color export styles – ideal for stable long-term repeat orders

Gaoyang Zone (42 exhibitors – Kitchen & Bath Textile Cluster)
• Core Products: Towels, bath towels, shower curtains, kitchen table runners, embroidered gift textiles
• Best For: Cross-border independent stores, East Asian retail channels, gift wholesalers, small-batch custom buyers
• Export Advantages: Flexible production lines, low MOQ options, ample light-colored printed designs – well-suited for cross-border small trial orders and fast SKU iteration

Huzhou & Haining Zone (38 exhibitors – Premium Upholstery & Curtain Fabric Cluster)
• Core Products: Blackout jacquard curtains, sofa fabrics, textured decorative fabrics, soft furnishing accessories
• Best For: European interior contractors, mid-to-high-end soft furnishing brands, boutique home retailers
• Export Advantages: High proportion of matte-textured jacquard fabrics, mature wide-width weaving capabilities, strong value-add potential – key sourcing zone for premium differentiated soft furnishings

Qingdao Zone (33 exhibitors – Functional Knit & Baby Textile Cluster)
• Core Products: Infant knit fabrics, temperature-regulating blankets, anti-static/antibacterial functional home textiles
• Best For: EU/US baby product sellers, compliance-focused brand buyers
• Export Advantages: Fully compliant with EU/US infant safety standards, abundant solid-color minimalist designs, complete testing documentation – minimal customs clearance risk

Pujiang Zone (26 exhibitors – Pet Textile Cluster)
• Core Products: Pet mats, quilted pet blankets, fabric pet accessories
• Best For: Cross-border pet vertical sellers, overseas pet stores
• Export Advantages: China's most concentrated pet textile cluster, rapid design turnover, mature washing processes, high compliance rates – core sourcing hub for the cross-border pet blue ocean category

Xiaoshan Zone (15 exhibitors – Premium Filling Materials & Upstream Cluster)
• Core Products: Down and silk fillings, eco-friendly washable filling materials, semi-finished home textile fabrics
• Best For: In-house manufacturing brands, premium home textile brands, raw material traders
• Export Advantages: Upstream supply chain with focus on natural sustainable materials – ideal for bespoke finished goods and private-label product development

Northern Wool Cluster Zone (21 exhibitors – Gift & Outdoor Blanket Cluster)
• Core Products: Vintage jacquard camping blankets, earth-tone decorative throws, festive plush textile gifts
• Best For: Overseas festive gift wholesalers, outdoor & casual home retailers, seasonal stock-up buyers
• Export Advantages: Wide variety of differentiated designs, higher per-unit margins – well-suited for EU/US festive, outdoor, and home gift niche markets

International Pavilion (74 overseas exhibitors – Premium Imported Fabrics)
• Core Products: Uzbek cotton yarns, Portuguese linen fabrics, Japanese fine bath textiles
• Best For: Premium concept stores, niche natural-material brands, buyers seeking differentiated imported materials
• Export Advantages: Differentiated natural-material positioning – ideal for building premium product lines


II. Quick Reference – Match Your Sourcing Focus to the Right Zone

① Bulk bedding, hotel linens → Nantong

② Kitchen & bath textiles, small cross‑border trial orders → Gaoyang

③ Curtains, upholstery, premium soft furnishings → Huzhou & Haining

④ Baby & functional knit textiles with export compliance → Qingdao

⑤ Pet textiles & accessories → Pujiang

⑥ Filling materials, semi‑finished fabrics → Xiaoshan

⑦ Festive & outdoor blankets, seasonal gift items → Northern Wool Cluster

⑧ Premium imported fabrics & natural materials → International Pavilion
