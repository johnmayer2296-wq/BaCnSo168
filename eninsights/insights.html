Shipping from China: Basic Guide for Overseas Buyers

Four core steps for shipping from China: choose sea, air, express, or rail based on shipment volume and delivery urgency; understand trade terms like FOB and EXW to determine who pays freight and handles customs clearance; prepare invoices, packing lists, and bills of lading—ensuring all three documents are consistent; work with a professional freight forwarder and request an all‑inclusive price. Always allow buffer time in your schedule and steer clear of peak‑season surcharges.
